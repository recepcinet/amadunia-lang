# Sol lai

*The sun comes.*

The fifteenth text, and the lowest rung on [the reading
ladder](../lessons/reading-ladder.md): it uses **only the 89 roots the lessons
have taught by Lesson 10**, and no rule beyond that lesson.

Ten lessons in, a learner has no prepositions, no *ini* or *itu*, no verb
chains, no comparison and no subordinate clauses — those arrive between Lessons
12 and 18. What is there is a subject, a verb, an object, an adjective after
its noun, the two tense markers, the plural, possession, a question and a
command. This page is what that is enough for.

---

```
Sol lai. Din hao.

Mama mi suda kupi pan.
Papa mi nomu ca.
Mama sema: "Kula pan!"

Anak keci lala. Mi kan ta.
Mi sema: "Sasa, anak! Sol lai."

Anak ambil mano mi. Kita go market.

Market kabir. Cok insan situ.
Mi kupi ca. Anak kan foto.
Pesa mi keci sasa.

Rafiki mi lai. Ta sema: "Hi! Yu hao?"
Mi sema: "Ya. Familia mi hao."
Ta sema: "Yu suru ke?"
Mi sema: "Mi suru rabota."

Kita go dom.
Rat lai. Familia mi sini.
Anak lala.
```

## What ten lessons are enough for

Twenty-three sentences and 42 roots. A morning, an errand, a meeting, an
evening — the shape of a day, told with no rule the course has not reached.

**Four things the page had to do without**, and none of them is missing from
the language:

| Not yet taught | Arrives in | Written instead |
|---|---|---|
| *in*, *dari*, *por* | Lesson 15 | *Kita go market* — a movement verb takes its target bare, so going somewhere works; **being** somewhere does not |
| *ini*, *itu* | Lesson 15 | nothing is pointed at; the photograph is *foto*, never *this photograph* |
| verb chains | Lesson 17 | *Mi suru rabota*, not *Mi mau suru rabota* |
| *porke*, *kab* as joiners | Lesson 18 | every sentence stands alone; nothing is because of anything |

**The last line of the page is where the absence shows.** *My family is at
home* cannot be written at Lesson 10: *dom* is a noun, a noun predicate needs
*es*, and *Familia mi es dom* would say my family is a house. The page says
*Familia mi sini* — my family is here — which is true, and is not the same
sentence.

**Nothing is because of anything.** Without *porke* the child wakes, the market
is crowded and the money runs short, and the page cannot say that one follows
from another. That is the single largest difference between this text and
[text 14](text-14-yamur-aur-ca.md), eight lessons later.
