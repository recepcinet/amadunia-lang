# Tarik por hospital

*The way to the hospital.*

The twenty-fifth text, and the first written as **directions to a stranger**.
The phrasebook has the fragments — *Hotel nali?*, *Go yamin* — and no page has
ever put them together into somebody actually finding a place.

It was written for two reasons. Commands stand in seven texts, level with
comparison at the floor of the exercise table, and directions are made of them.
And [text 24 found that nothing can be near anything](text-24-mama-mama-mi.md):
the language has three prepositions and none of them is *beside*. A page about
where things are is the hardest test that gap can be given, and a second
witness is worth more than a second argument.

Written at 300 roots, with no grammar beyond Lesson 26.

---

```
— Pardon. Hospital nali?
— Hospital baid. Yu anda o yu go in auto?

— Mi anda.
— Ok. Anda tarik ini. Go yamin in ponte.

— Ponte cang?
— Ya. Sungai sub.

— Mi kan market kabir sasa.
— Hao. Hospital baid dari market.
— Berapa tempo?
— Des momen agar yu anda hayai.

— Posta nali?
— No go por posta. Posta kiri, hospital yamin.

— Mi no faham. Sema tena, plis.
— Anda tarik cang. No go kiri. Go yamin.

— Ok. Mersi!
— Hospital kabir aur putih. Yu saufa kan ta.
```

## Line by line

| Amadunia | English |
|---|---|
| — Pardon. Hospital nali? | — Excuse me. Where is the hospital? |
| — Hospital baid. | — The hospital is far. |
| Yu anda o yu go in auto? | Are you walking or going by car? |
| — Mi anda. | — I'm walking. |
| — Ok. Anda tarik ini. | — Ok. Walk this road. |
| Go yamin in ponte. | Turn right at the bridge. |
| — Ponte cang? | — Is the bridge long? |
| — Ya. Sungai sub. | — Yes. The river is below. |
| — Mi kan market kabir sasa. | — I see a big market now. |
| — Hao. Hospital baid dari market. | — Good. The hospital is far from the market. |
| — Berapa tempo? | — How much time? |
| — Des momen agar yu anda hayai. | — Ten moments if you walk fast. |
| — Posta nali? | — Where is the post? |
| — No go por posta. | — Do not go to the post. |
| Posta kiri, hospital yamin. | The post is left, the hospital right. |
| — Mi no faham. Sema tena, plis. | — I don't understand. Say it again, please. |
| — Anda tarik cang. No go kiri. Go yamin. | — Walk the long road. Do not go left. Go right. |
| — Ok. Mersi! | — Ok. Thank you! |
| — Hospital kabir aur putih. | — The hospital is big and white. |
| Yu saufa kan ta. | You will see it. |

## What directions are made of

**Seven of the twenty-eight sentences are commands**, and three of the seven
are negative. *No go kiri* and *No go por posta* carry as much of this page as
*Go yamin* does — a direction is as often about where not to turn — and no
other text has needed the negative command more than once.

The rest is the question rule doing the other half. *Hospital nali?*, *Posta
nali?*, *Berapa tempo?* — the question word stands where the answer will
stand, so the answer is the question with one word swapped, and a stranger who
learns the question has learned the answer.

## Gaps

**Nothing is beside anything, for the second time.** *Sungai sub* says the
river is below and stops there; the sentence a speaker wants is *the river is
below the bridge*, and *sub* cannot take a noun any more than *dekat* could in
[text 24](text-24-mama-mama-mi.md). This page had to put the bridge in one
sentence and the river in the next and let the reader join them.
[The rule page records it](../grammar/place.md) as a missing slot rather than a
missing word, and this is the second text to run into it in two days — the
first by accident, this one on purpose.

**There is no word for a corner, a turn or a crossing.** *Go yamin in ponte* is
"go right at the bridge", and a bridge is the only landmark this page can use
because the language has one — [the house and the city are the two thinnest
domains on the checklist](../dictionary/a1-checklist.md). Directions in any
city are given by corners.

**No word for *far* that is not also a judgement.** *baid* is far, and *Hospital
baid* is the first thing the stranger is told; *ten moments* has to do the work
of saying how far, because a distance the language can name does not exist.
[Recorded with the other things the writing has asked
for](../dictionary/README.md#words-the-writing-has-asked-for).

**And *ten moments* is not a time.** *momen* is a moment and *des momen* is ten
of them, which is a number of an unmeasured thing. [The clock has no
word](../dictionary/README.md#words-the-writing-has-asked-for) and neither has
the minute, so the only honest answer to *how much time* is a guess dressed as
a count.
