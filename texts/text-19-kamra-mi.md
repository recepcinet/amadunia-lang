# Kamra mi

*My room.*

The nineteenth text, written for the largest gap left on [the reading
ladder](../lessons/reading-ladder.md): between Lesson 18 and Lesson 24 there
was nothing to read. It uses **only the roots taught by Lesson 22** — 256 of them — and Lesson 21
is where a room and its things arrive, so a learner who has just finished that
lesson reads a page made almost entirely of what they have just learned. The
one word that carries it past 21 is *pagi*, morning, which the course did not
teach until Lesson 22; [the ladder](../lessons/reading-ladder.md) computed 22
and the draft of this page had claimed 21.

---

```
Kamra mi keci. Es uan pencere.

Pencere kabir. Mi kan pohon aur asman.
In pagi sol lai in kamra.

Es yatak, tabla aur kursi.
Libro, kertas aur lampu in tabla.
Lampu mi eski. Ta beri luma hao.

Mi kara libro in yatak.
Mi katab surat in tabla.
Kalima mi keci, ide mi kabir.

Flor in pencere. Flor merah aur putih.
Mi beri akua por flor in pagi.

Rafiki mi lai. Kita nomu ca aur sema.
Kamra keci. Kita tri sini.

In rat mi funga pencere. Kamra barid.
Mi lala hao.

Kamra ini keci. Mi ama kamra mi.
```

## What Lesson 21 is enough for

Twenty-four sentences, 49 roots, and no rule the course has not reached: the
existential, possession, a subordinate-free description, an adverb, a
conjunction. The vocabulary is the lesson's own — *kamra*, *pencere*, *lampu*,
*yatak*, *kertas*, *kalima*, *ide*, *flor* — which is the point of writing at
this rung rather than at 300.

## Gaps

**A room with no walls and no floor.** The dictionary has a house, a room, a
door, a window, a bed, a table, a chair and a lamp, and not one surface to put
them against. *Kamra mi keci* is as close as the language gets to describing
the shape of a room. Three words are missing and nothing here can stand in for
them, so the description goes around the room's contents and never touches the
room. Recorded as [a gap the writing asked
for](../dictionary/README.md#words-the-writing-has-asked-for).

**No word for *every*.** *Mi beri akua por flor in pagi* says "in the morning",
where a person waters plants **every** morning. *all, some, none* is
[on the open list](../grammar/plural.md) and this is the first page to want the
fourth member of that family.

**No word for *but*, a sixth time.** *Kamra keci. Kita tri sini* is two
sentences where one was meant: the room is small **but** the three of us fit.
The draft of this page wrote it with *lakin*, the candidate in
[the briefing](../grammar/proposal-but.md), to see how the sentence would look
if the briefing were accepted — and [`check.py`](../check.py) rejected it,
correctly. **A text invents nothing.** A briefing is where a word that does not
exist may be written; a text is where the absence has to be felt. The line
stands as two sentences, and the gap is recorded rather than papered over.
