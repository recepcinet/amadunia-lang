## A conversation

> — Yu senang o sedih?
> — Mi senang! Mi suda naiti rabota baru.
> — Nali?
> — In skola baru. Mi saufa es mualim anak-anak.
> — Muskil?
> — Ya, muskil. Aur hao. Mi ama anak-anak.
> — Mi senti yu saufa es mualim paling hao.

*Are you happy or sad? — I'm happy! I've found a new job. — Where? — At a new
school. I'll be the children's teacher. — Difficult? — Yes, difficult. And
good. I love children. — I feel you will be the best teacher.*

## Practice

1. Mi dengar golos yu. — *I hear your voice.*
2. Gelas ini plen, gelas itu kosong. — *This glass is full, that one is empty.*
3. Mi tanya, ta respon. — *I ask, she answers.*
4. Es bahaya porke tarik kotor. — *There is danger because the road is dirty.*
5. Mi senti mi bisa kanta! — *I feel I can sing!*
6. Natura, historia, kultura — tri ide kabir. — *Nature, history, culture — three big ideas.*
