# Lesson 24 — The table and the city

*Prerequisite: [Lesson 23](lesson-23-everything-so-far.md).*

These thirty words entered the dictionary when it passed 180 roots and never
got a lesson. This one and [the next](lesson-25-doing-and-being.md) close that
gap. No new grammar — everything here uses rules you already have.

## On the table

| | |
|---|---|
| Es gelas aur piring in tabla. | There is a glass and a plate on the table. |
| Sikin in tabla. | The knife is on the table. |
| Mi kula nyama aur risi. | I eat meat and rice. |
| Namak aur sukar putih. | Salt and sugar are white. |
| Ca lebi dulce dari kafe. | Tea is sweeter than coffee. |
| Mi nomu kafe in pagi. | I drink coffee in the morning. |
| Mi kupi fruta in market. | I buy fruit at the market. |

## The city

| | |
|---|---|
| Madina ini sundar. | This city is beautiful. |
| Es banka in madina ini? | Is there a bank in this city? |
| Hospital in tarik ini. | The hospital is on this road. |
| Mi kula in restoran. | I eat at a restaurant. |
| Ponte in sungai. | The bridge is over the river. |
| Tempat ini hao. | This place is good. |
| Negara mi kabir. | My country is big. |

## The sky at night

| | |
|---|---|
| Bulan aur stela in asman. | The moon and the stars are in the sky. |
| Bulan putih. Stela keci. | The moon is white. The stars are small. |
| Mi lihat bulan kab rat lai. | I look at the moon when night comes. |

## Time

| | |
|---|---|
| Uan hafta punya seti din. | One week has seven days. |
| Uan tahun punya pat mausim. | One year has four seasons. |
| Aksam lai. Sol go. | Evening comes. The sun goes. |
| Mi go skola besok. | I go to school tomorrow. |
| Mi suda rabota kemarin. | I worked yesterday. |
| Tempo go hayai. | Time goes fast. |
| Momen ini sundar. | This moment is beautiful. |

## A conversation

> — Yu mau kula in restoran besok?
> — Ya! Restoran nali?
> — Dekat ponte, in tarik kabir.
> — Ok. Mi lai in aksam.
> — Hao. Ta punya nyama, risi, fruta — aur kafe paling hao in madina.
> — Mi no nomu kafe. Mi nomu ca.
> — Ta punya ca tena!

*Do you want to eat at a restaurant tomorrow? — Yes! Where is the restaurant?
— Near the bridge, on the big road. — Ok. I'll come in the evening. — Good.
They have meat, rice, fruit — and the best coffee in the city. — I don't drink
coffee. I drink tea. — They have tea too!*

## Practice

1. Es sikin aur piring in tabla. — *There is a knife and a plate on the table.*
2. Madina ini punya du ponte. — *This city has two bridges.*
3. Mi lihat bulan aur stela. — *I look at the moon and the stars.*
4. Uan tahun punya des-du mensis aur pat mausim. — *A year has twelve months and four seasons.*
5. Nasab mi rabota in hospital. — *My relative works at a hospital.*
6. Kemarin aksam, besok pagi. — *Yesterday evening, tomorrow morning.*
