# Lesson 17 — Wanting, being able, having to

*Prerequisite: [Lesson 16](lesson-16-school-and-time.md).*

## New words

| Word | Meaning |
|---|---|
| bisa | can, to be able |
| lasim | must, to have to |

## Just put the verbs together

| | |
|---|---|
| Mi mau kula. | I want to eat. |
| Mi mau kula pan. | I want to eat bread. |
| Yu bisa otur. | You can sit. |
| Yu bisa otur sini. | You can sit here. |
| Ta lasim go skola. | She must go to school. |
| Kita lasim funga libro. | We must close the book. |

There is no word for "to". Nothing goes between the verbs, and neither verb changes.

Until now you could only say *Mi mau pan* — "I want bread". Now you can want to **do** things.

## Past and future

The time word goes in front of the first verb:

| | |
|---|---|
| Mi suda mau kula. | I wanted to eat. |
| Yu saufa bisa kara. | You will be able to read. |
| Ta suda lasim rabota. | She had to work. |

## Saying no

*no* also goes in front:

| | |
|---|---|
| Mi no mau kula. | I do not want to eat. |
| Ta no bisa lai. | She cannot come. |
| Yu no lasim go. | You do not have to go. |
| Mi no suda mau studi. | I did not want to study. |

## Moving *no* moves the meaning

You already know *no* goes in front of what it denies. Put it in a different place and it denies a different thing:

| | |
|---|---|
| Mi **no** mau kula. | I do not want to eat. |
| Mi mau **no** kula. | I want *not* to eat. |

Nothing new to learn — the old rule, applied where there are now two verbs.

## Three verbs

| | |
|---|---|
| Mi mau studi kara. | I want to learn to read. |
| Yu bisa studi katab. | You can learn to write. |
| Kita lasim studi faham. | We must learn to understand. |

There is no limit. The verbs line up, the object goes last.

## A conversation

> — Salam! Yu mau go market una mi?
> — Mi no bisa. Mi lasim studi.
> — Yu studi ke?
> — Mi studi Amadunia. Mi mau kara libro ini.
> — Libro cang?
> — Ya, cok cang. Mi lasim kara du hora.
> — Yu bisa kara in dom mi. Ca aur pan situ!
> — Mersi! Mi saufa lai tena.

*Hello! Do you want to go to the market with me? — I can't. I have to study. — What are you studying? — Amadunia. I want to read this book. — Is the book long? — Yes, very long. I have to read for two hours. — You can read at my house. Tea and bread there! — Thanks! I'll come again.*

## Practice

1. Mi mau nomu ca. — *I want to drink tea.*
2. Anak no bisa lala. — *The child cannot sleep.*
3. Kita lasim anda tena. — *We must walk again.*
4. Yu suda mau kupi libro itu? — *Did you want to buy that book?*
5. Ta bisa sema aur ta bisa kara. — *She can speak and she can read.*
6. Mi no lasim rabota din ini. — *I do not have to work today.*

## What you can already say

Seventeen lessons, **134 roots**. Want, can, must — and any verb after any of them. This was the last thing the grammar was missing.

**Still open:** how to say "I want *you* to eat" — a second subject inside the sentence; and "there is"; and "bigger than". See the open questions in [grammar/](../grammar/).
