## Eight new verbs

| | |
|---|---|
| Mi kirim surat por rafiki mi. | I send a letter to my friend. |
| Mi suda naiti anahtar mi! | I found my key! |

## Remember and forget

They are opposites and they behave the same:

| | |
|---|---|
| Mi ingat yu daima. | I always remember you. |
| Mi no unut yu. | I do not forget you. |
| Ta suda unut kalima ini. | She forgot this word. |

## True and false

| | |
|---|---|
| Habari ini benar. | This news is true. |
| Habari itu es yalan. | That news is a lie. |
| Mi no sema yalan. | I do not tell lies. |

## A conversation

> — Pagi hao! Yu ingat kita go hutan din ini?
> — Mi suda unut! Mi lasim gasil aur kula.
> — Mi tabak corba. Yu kula, sasa kita go.
> — Mersi! Kanis mi lai una kita?
> — Ya. Ta genc aur ta mau kimbia.
> — Hutan itu amik. Mi punya keci korku.
> — Mi es una yu. No es hatari — mi ingat tarik.

*Good morning! Do you remember we're going to the forest today? — I forgot! I have to wash and eat. — I'll cook soup. You eat, then we go. — Thanks! Is my dog coming with us? — Yes. He's young and he wants to run. — That forest is deep. I have a little fear. — I'm with you. There's no danger — I remember the way.*

## Practice

1. Mi suda naiti libro mi in kamra. — *I found my book in the room.*
2. Kanis no bisa kara masal. — *A dog cannot read a story.*
3. Petra sakil, kertas hafif. — *Stone is heavy, paper is light.*
4. Mi ingat cehra ta aur golos ta. — *I remember her face and her voice.*
5. Kita finis oyun kab pagi lai. — *We finish the game when morning comes.*
6. Mimpi mi es yalan o benar? — *Is my dream a lie or true?*
