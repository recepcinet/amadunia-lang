# Lesson 1 — Greetings

*Everything in this lesson uses only settled vocabulary.*

## New words

| Word | Meaning |
|---|---|
| hi | hello |
| salam | peaceful greeting |
| ok | ok |
| ya | yes |
| no | no |
| bai | goodbye |

## Your first conversation

> — Hi! Ok?
> — Ya, ok! Bai!

Literally: *Hello! Ok?* — *Yes, ok! Goodbye!*

That is a complete, correct Amadunia exchange. There is nothing hidden in it: no conjugation to learn, no gendered form to choose, no article to place.

## Two ways to greet

*hi* is the everyday greeting — quick, neutral, usable with anyone.

*salam* carries the older sense of wishing peace. Use it when you want the greeting to mean something.

> — Salam!
> — Salam! Ok?
> — Ya, ok.

## Saying no

*no* answers exactly as *ya* does, in the same slot:

> — Ok?
> — No.

## Practice

Say these out loud. Every letter has one sound, so read them exactly as written.

1. Hi!
2. Salam! Ok?
3. Ya, ok! Bai!
4. Hi! ... No. ... Bai!

## What you can already say

With six words you can open a conversation, ask how someone is, answer either way, and close. That is the whole point of the design.
