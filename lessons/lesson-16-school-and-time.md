# Lesson 16 — School and time

*Prerequisite: [Lesson 15](lesson-15-pointing-placing.md).*

| kursi | chair |

| hora | hour |

## At school

| | |
|---|---|
| Mi studi in skola. | I study at school. |
| Skola ini kabir. | This school is big. |
| Anak-anak in skola sasa. | The children are at school now. |
| Mi kara libro in skola. | I read a book at school. |

## Books and chairs

| | |
|---|---|
| Libro ini sundar. | This book is beautiful. |
| Mi otur in kursi. | I sit on a chair. |
| Libro itu por dugu mi. | That book is for my sibling. |
| Mi suda kupi libro dari market. | I bought a book from the market. |

## Thinking

| | |
|---|---|
| Mi fikir. | I think. |
| Mi fikir cok. | I think a lot. |
| Yu fikir ke? | What do you think? |
| Ta suda fikir aur ta suda faham. | She thought and she understood. |

## Open and close

*buka* you have had since Lesson 13. Its pair arrives now:

| | |
|---|---|
| Mi buka libro. | I open the book. |
| Mi funga libro. | I close the book. |
| Buka ain yu! | Open your eyes! |
| Skola funga rat ini. | The school closes tonight. |

## Again

| | |
|---|---|
| Mi lai tena. | I come again. |
| Sema tena, plis. | Say it again, please. |
| Rafiki mi saufa lai tena. | My friend will come again. |
| Mi no studi tena. | I do not study again. |

*tena* goes at the end, where time words go.

## Long and short, more

| | |
|---|---|
| Tarik ini cang. | This road is long. |
| Tarik itu duan. | That road is short. |
| Din cang, rat duan. | The day is long, the night is short. |
| Mi mau lebi ca. | I want more tea. |
| Lebi libro, plis! | More books, please! |

*lebi* goes before the noun, exactly where *cok* goes.

## Hours and years

| | |
|---|---|
| Uan hora. | One hour. |
| Tri hora. | Three hours. |
| Mi suda studi du tahun. | I studied for two years. |
| Anak itu fai tahun. | That child is five years old. |

Remember: after a number the noun stays single. *Tri hora*, never *tri hora-hora*.

## Rain

| | |
|---|---|
| Yamur lai. | Rain comes. |
| Yamur barid. | The rain is cold. |
| Mi no go market — yamur! | I'm not going to the market — rain! |
| Yamur aur angin din ini. | Rain and wind today. |

## A conversation

> — Salam! Yu go nali?
> — Mi go skola. Yu?
> — Mi in dom. Yamur situ!
> — Ya, yamur aur angin. Tarik dari dom por skola cang.
> — Berapa hora?
> — Du hora. Mi no anda in yamur.
> — Yu otur in dom aur yu kara libro.
> — Libro ke?
> — Libro ini! Mi suda kara tena aur tena. Libro hao.
> — Mersi! Mi saufa kara sasa.

*Hello! Where are you going? — To school. You? — I'm at home. Rain out there! — Yes, rain and wind. The road from home to school is long. — How many hours? — Two hours. I don't walk in the rain. — You sit at home and you read a book. — Which book? — This book! I've read it again and again. It's a good book. — Thanks! I'll read it now.*

## Practice

1. Mi kara libro in skola din ini. — *I read a book at school today.*
2. Tarik dari market por dom duan. — *The road from the market to home is short.*
3. Yu fikir ke? — *What do you think?*
4. Funga libro aur otur. — *Close the book and sit.*
5. Mi no suda studi tahun itu. — *I did not study that year.*
6. Lebi ca, plis. Aur pan! — *More tea, please. And bread!*

## What you can already say

Sixteen lessons, **132 roots**. School, books, roads, hours, years, rain — and *tena*, which lets anything happen twice.

**A word this lesson could not add.** *bisa* — "can, be able" — was chosen for this batch and dropped, because *Yu bisa otur* would have put one verb in front of another and that had never been decided. It was settled the next day: see [Lesson 17](lesson-17-wanting-and-able.md). Every lesson up to this one keeps to one verb at a time.
