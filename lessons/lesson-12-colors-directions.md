# Lesson 12 — Colours and directions

*Prerequisite: [Lesson 11](lesson-11-being.md).*

## New words

| Word | Meaning |
|---|---|
| merah | red |
| asul | blue |
| upar | up, above |
| sub | down, below |
| baru | new |
| eski | old |
| hayai | fast |
| dugu | sibling |
| kara | to read |
| katab | to write |
| anda | to walk |
| musik | music |

## Colours describe, so they take no *es*

A colour is an adjective. It follows the noun, and nothing stands between them.

| | |
|---|---|
| Ruma merah. | The house is red. |
| Akua asul. | The water is blue. |
| Ca mi merah. | My tea is red. |
| Mi kan telefon asul. | I see a blue telephone. |

Compare with a noun, which does take *es*:

| | |
|---|---|
| Sol es doktor. | Sol is a doctor. |
| Sol merah. | Sol is red. |

## Up and down

| | |
|---|---|
| Sol upar. | The sun is above. |
| Anak sub. | The child is below. |
| Familia mi upar. | My family is upstairs. |
| Ain mi kan upar. | My eyes look up. |

## New and old

| | |
|---|---|
| Telefon mi baru. | My telephone is new. |
| Hotel eski. | The hotel is old. |
| Mi suda kupi ruma baru. | I bought a new house. |
| Rafiki eski hao. | An old friend is good. |

Two adjectives in a row? Put the owner first, then describe: *ruma mi baru* — my new house.

## Reading and writing

*kara* and *katab* come from the same tradition: *kara* gave the world *Quran* — "that which is read" — and *katab* gave *kitab*, the word for "book" in Arabic, Turkish, Hindi, Urdu, Swahili and Indonesian alike.

| | |
|---|---|
| Mi kara. | I read. |
| Yu katab ke? | What are you writing? |
| Anak suda kara. | The child read. |
| Doktor saufa katab nama mi. | The doctor will write my name. |

## Walking, fast

| | |
|---|---|
| Mi anda market. | I walk to the market. |
| Ta anda hayai. | She walks fast. |
| Kita suda anda cok. | We walked a lot. |
| Taksi hayai. | The taxi is fast. |

## Sibling — one word for both

*dugu* is a brother **or** a sister, exactly as *ta* is he or she. Amadunia does not split people by gender anywhere.

| | |
|---|---|
| Dugu mi es doktor. | My sibling is a doctor. |
| Dugu-dugu mi situ. | My siblings are there. |
| Mi es dugu yu. | I am your sibling. |

## A conversation

> — Salam! Ruma yu nali?
> — Ruma mi situ, upar. Ruma merah.
> — Ruma baru?
> — No, eski. Ma hao!
> — Dugu yu situ?
> — Ya. Ta kara, mi katab. Kita ama musik.

*Hello! Where is your house? — My house is there, above. The red house. — Is it new? — No, old. But good! — Is your sibling there? — Yes. She reads, I write. We love music.*

## Practice

1. Telefon mi es baru. — *careful:* **wrong.** *baru* is an adjective — say *Telefon mi baru.*
2. Sol upar, anak sub. — *The sun is above, the child below.*
3. Yu kara ke? — *What are you reading?*
4. Dugu mi anda hayai. — *My sibling walks fast.*
5. Mi suda katab nama yu. — *I wrote your name.*
6. Musik yu merah? — *Is your music red?* (nonsense, but grammatical)

## What you can already say

Twelve lessons, **101 roots** — past one hundred. You can colour things, place them above and below, call them new or old or fast, read, write, walk, and talk about your sibling without ever naming a gender.
