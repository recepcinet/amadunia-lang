# Lesson 14 — Not, and

*Prerequisite: [Lesson 13](lesson-13-weather-directions.md).*

## New words

| Word | Meaning |
|---|---|
| aur | and |

One new word. The other half of this lesson is a word you have had since Lesson 1.

## *no* was waiting all along

You have used *no* to answer since your first conversation. It does a second job with no change at all: put it in front of what you are denying.

| | |
|---|---|
| Mi kula pan. | I eat bread. |
| Mi **no** kula pan. | I do not eat bread. |
| Mi es doktor. | I am a doctor. |
| Mi **no** es doktor. | I am not a doctor. |
| Dom kabir. | The house is big. |
| Dom **no** kabir. | The house is not big. |
| Mi sini. | I am here. |
| Mi **no** sini. | I am not here. |

There is no separate negative form of any verb. *kula* stays *kula*.

## With past and future

*no* comes first, then the time word:

| | |
|---|---|
| Mi **no suda** kula. | I did not eat. |
| Yu **no saufa** lai. | You will not come. |
| Ta **no suda es** anak. | She was not a child. |

The order never changes: **no → suda/saufa → verb**.

## Answering, as before

> — Yu mau ca?
> — No.

> — Yu mau ca?
> — No, mi no mau ca. Mi mau akua.

## *aur* joins

| | |
|---|---|
| Mi kupi pan **aur** ca. | I buy bread and tea. |
| Angin **aur** yuki lai. | Wind and snow came. |
| Dom mi keci **aur** sundar. | My house is small and beautiful. |
| Mi lai **aur** mi kan yu. | I came and I saw you. |

Nouns, adjectives, whole sentences — one word for all three.

For a list, put *aur* between each pair:

> Mi kupi pan **aur** ca **aur** akua.

In writing you may use commas for all but the last: *pan, ca aur akua*. The full form is never wrong.

## Why you could not just say *pan ca*

Because you already learned what that means. Two nouns together is possession — *pan ca* is "the tea's bread". The slot was taken, so "and" needed a word of its own.

## A conversation

> — Salam! Yu mau ca aur pan?
> — Mi mau ca. Mi no mau pan, mersi.
> — Yu no kula sasa?
> — No. Mi suda kula. Mi mau ca aur akua.
> — Ok. Ca garam aur akua barid.
> — Mersi! Yu es rafiki hao.

*Hello! Do you want tea and bread? — I want tea. I don't want bread, thanks. — You're not eating now? — No. I ate. I want tea and water. — Ok. Hot tea and cold water. — Thanks! You're a good friend.*

## Practice

1. Mi no faham. — *I do not understand.*
2. Anak no suda lala. — *The child did not sleep.*
3. Sol aur luma. — *The sun and the light.*
4. Dom mi no kabir aur no keci. — *My house is not big and not small.*
5. Yu no es doktor? — *Are you not a doctor?*
6. Mi studi aur mi faham. — *I study and I understand.*

## What you can already say

Fourteen lessons, **118 roots**. With one new word and one old word doing new work, you can now deny anything and join anything — the two things a language cannot do without.
