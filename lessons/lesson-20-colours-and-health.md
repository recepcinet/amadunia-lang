## Four more colours

Colours follow the noun, like every adjective, and take no *es*:

| | |
|---|---|
| Rambut mi siya. | My hair is black. |
| Yuki putih. | Snow is white. |
| Sol asfar. | The sun is yellow. |
| Pohon yesil. | The tree is green. |
| Mi mau kamisa asul, no kamisa merah. | I want a blue shirt, not a red one. |

## Sky and cloud

| | |
|---|---|
| Asman asul din ini. | The sky is blue today. |
| Es cok badal. | There are many clouds. |
| Badal siya — yamur lai! | Black clouds — rain is coming! |
| Asman lebi kabir dari bahari. | The sky is bigger than the sea. |

## More body

| | |
|---|---|
| Rambut ta cang aur siya. | Her hair is long and black. |
| Mi punya du telinga aur uan mulut. | I have two ears and one mouth. |
| Dente anak keci putih. | The small child's teeth are white. |

## Being ill

| | |
|---|---|
| Mi bimar. | I am sick. |
| Ta suda bimar, sasa ta hao. | She was sick, now she is well. |
| Doktor beri ilac por mi. | The doctor gives medicine to me. |
| Mi no bisa go skola porke mi bimar. | I can't go to school because I'm sick. |

## Work

| | |
|---|---|
| Papa mi es kisan. | My father is a farmer. |
| Tacir itu beca fruta in market. | That merchant sells fruit at the market. |
| Sofer punya auto merah. | The driver has a red car. |
| Mualim mi paling hao. | My teacher is the best. |

## Food

| | |
|---|---|
| Mi nomu halib daima. | I always drink milk. |
| Es telur aur legum in dom? | Are there eggs and vegetables at home? |
| Anak-anak kula telur kadang. | The children sometimes eat eggs. |

*Where *daima* and *kadang* stand is [not settled](../grammar/proposal-frequency.md); this course uses three positions and none of them is the rule yet.*

## Life and freedom

| | |
|---|---|
| Umur mi sundar. | My life is beautiful. |
| Uhuru es ide paling kabir. | Freedom is the biggest idea. |
| Mi mau uhuru por insan-insan. | I want freedom for people. |

## A conversation

> — Salam! Yu hao?
> — No, mi bimar. Sar mi garam.
> — Yu suda go doktor?
> — Ya. Ta suda beri ilac por mi. Mi lasim nomu ilac tri din.
> — Mi saufa kupi halib aur telur por yu.
> — Mersi! Yu es rafiki paling hao.
> — Mi lai besok. Agar yu lebi hao, kita suru oyun.

*Hello! Are you well? — No, I'm sick. My head is hot. — Did you go to the doctor? — Yes. She gave me medicine. I have to drink the medicine for three days. — I'll buy milk and eggs for you. — Thanks! You're the best friend. — I'll come tomorrow. If you're better, we'll play a game.*

## Practice

1. Badal siya in asman. — *Black clouds in the sky.*
2. Mi no bimar sasa. — *I am not sick now.*
3. Kisan itu punya cok animal. — *That farmer has many animals.*
4. Telinga mi keci, mulut mi kabir. — *My ears are small, my mouth is big.*
5. Mi katab kalima ini in surat. — *I write this word in the letter.*
6. Uhuru lebi hao dari pesa. — *Freedom is better than money.*

## What you can already say

Twenty lessons, **207 roots**. Six colours, the sky and its clouds, being ill and getting better, four kinds of work, and the first large ideas the language can hold — *umur*, *uhuru*, *benar*.

What is still open is kept in one place: [the open questions](../grammar/README.md).
