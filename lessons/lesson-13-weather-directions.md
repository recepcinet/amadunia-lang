# Lesson 13 — Weather and directions

*Prerequisite: [Lesson 12](lesson-12-colors-directions.md).*

## New words

| Word | Meaning |
|---|---|
| angin | wind |
| yuki | snow |
| yamin | right |
| kiri | left |
| cang | long |
| sundar | beautiful |
| asan | easy |
| buka | to open |
| faham | to understand |
| studi | to learn |
| espera | to wait |
| kimbia | to run |

## Weather

Weather words are nouns. Describe them with an adjective and no *es*:

| | |
|---|---|
| Angin barid. | The wind is cold. |
| Yuki asul. | The snow is blue. |
| Din garam, rat barid. | The day is hot, the night is cold. |
| Yuki sundar! | The snow is beautiful! |

But say **what** something is, and *es* comes back:

| | |
|---|---|
| Yuki es akua. | Snow is water. |

## Right and left

| | |
|---|---|
| Dom mi yamin. | My house is on the right. |
| Market kiri. | The market is on the left. |
| Mano yamin, mano kiri. | Right hand, left hand. |
| Anda yamin, plis. | Walk right, please. |

*yamin* comes from Arabic *yamīn*, "the right hand" — the same word that named **Yemen**, the land on Arabia's right.

## Long and beautiful and easy

| | |
|---|---|
| Din cang. | The day is long. |
| Rat cang. Mi espera. | The night is long. I wait. |
| Dugu mi sundar. | My sibling is beautiful. |
| Amadunia asan! | Amadunia is easy! |

## Four new verbs

| | |
|---|---|
| Mi buka telefon. | I open the telephone. |
| Yu faham? | Do you understand? |
| Mi studi Amadunia. | I am learning Amadunia. |
| Kita espera dugu yu. | We are waiting for your sibling. |
| Anak kimbia hayai. | The child runs fast. |

*espera* is Spanish and Portuguese *esperar*, to wait and to hope — the same root that gave **Esperanto** its name, "one who hopes". Amadunia takes the word and keeps the hope.

## With tense

Nothing new. The particle goes before the verb, and the verb never changes:

| | |
|---|---|
| Mi suda studi. | I learned. |
| Yu saufa faham. | You will understand. |
| Ta-ta suda espera cok. | They waited a lot. |
| Anak suda kimbia sub. | The child ran down. |

## A conversation

> — Salam! Yu faham Amadunia?
> — Ya! Mi studi sasa. Amadunia asan.
> — Hao! Dom yu nali?
> — Dom mi yamin, upar. Dom merah.
> — Angin barid sasa. Yuki?
> — Ya, yuki. Yuki sundar!
> — Kita anda? Mi espera yu.
> — Ok! Mi kimbia!

*Hello! Do you understand Amadunia? — Yes! I'm learning now. Amadunia is easy. — Good! Where is your house? — My house is on the right, above. The red house. — The wind is cold now. Snow? — Yes, snow. The snow is beautiful! — Shall we walk? I'll wait for you. — Ok! I'm running!*

## Practice

1. Angin hayai. — *The wind is fast.*
2. Yu faham mi? — *Do you understand me?*
3. Market yamin, hotel kiri. — *The market is on the right, the hotel on the left.*
4. Mi suda studi din cang. — *I studied a long day.*
5. Buka ain yu! — *Open your eyes!*
6. Dugu mi kimbia hayai. — *My sibling runs fast.*

## What you can already say

Thirteen lessons, **117 roots**. Weather, directions, and four verbs that carry a lot: open, understand, learn, wait. You can now say you are learning the language, in the language.
