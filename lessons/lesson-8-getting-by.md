# Lesson 8 — Getting by

*Prerequisite: [Lesson 7](lesson-7-questions.md).*

## New words

| Word | Meaning |
|---|---|
| mersi | thank you |
| plis | please |
| pardon | sorry, excuse me |
| madad | help |
| pesa | money |
| market | market, shop |
| rabota | work |
| insan | person |
| sini | here |
| sasa | now |
| garam | hot |
| barid | cold |

Most of these you already know. *mersi*, *pardon*, *plis*, *market* travel the world as they are; *insan*, *madad*, *pesa* are shared across a dozen languages each. Amadunia only wrote them down.

## The three words that open every door

| | |
|---|---|
| Plis. | Please. |
| Mersi. | Thank you. |
| Pardon. | Sorry. / Excuse me. |

They work alone, and they work at either end of a sentence:

> — Pardon! Market nali?
> — Market sini.
> — Mersi!

*Excuse me! Where is the market? — The market is here. — Thank you!*

## Asking for help

> — Madad! Plis!
> — Ke?
> — Mi mau doktor.
> — Ok. Doktor saufa lai.

*Help! Please! — What? — I want a doctor. — Ok. A doctor will come.*

## At the market

> — Salam! Yu mau ke?
> — Mi mau ca garam. Berapa pesa?
> — Du pesa.
> — Ok. Mersi!

*Hello! What do you want? — I want hot tea. How much money? — Two pesa. — Ok. Thanks!*

*berapa pesa* — literally "how many money" — is the whole of asking a price.

## Here and now

| | |
|---|---|
| Mi sini. | I am here. |
| Rafiki mi sini? | Is my friend here? |
| Mi mau ca sasa. | I want tea now. |
| Doktor saufa lai sasa. | The doctor will come now. |

## Hot and cold

| | |
|---|---|
| Ca garam. | The tea is hot. |
| Akua barid. | The water is cold. |
| Din garam, rat barid. | The day is hot, the night is cold. |
| Mi mau akua barid, plis. | I want cold water, please. |

## Work and people

| | |
|---|---|
| Mi rabota sini. | I work here. |
| Yu rabota nali? | Where do you work? |
| Insan hao. | A good person. / The person is good. |
| Cok insan sini. | Many people here. |

## Practice

1. Pardon, yu bil market nali? — *Excuse me, do you know where the market is?*
2. Mersi, rafiki! — *Thanks, friend!*
3. Mi mau madad, plis. — *I want help, please.*
4. Berapa pesa? — Tri pesa. — *How much? — Three.*
5. Insan-insan rabota sasa. — *The people are working now.*
6. Ca yu garam? — No, barid. — *Is your tea hot? — No, cold.*

## What you can already say

Eight lessons, 76 roots. You can now be polite, ask for help, find a place, ask a price, say where you are and when, and describe heat and cold — enough to get through a day somewhere you have never been.

**A note on word order.** *sini* and *sasa* are placed at the end of the sentence here, as *kab* and *porke* were in Lesson 7. Where time and place words go has not been settled on its own; see the open question in [grammar/questions.md](../grammar/questions.md).
