# Lesson 11 — Being

*Prerequisite: [Lesson 10](lesson-10-a-day.md).*

## New word

| Word | Meaning |
|---|---|
| es | is, am, are — before a noun |

One word. It is the most widely shared word on Earth: Spanish *es*, English *is*, German *ist*, Russian *est'*, Hindi *hai*, Persian *ast*, Latin *est*. You already know it.

## Before a noun: es

| | |
|---|---|
| Mi es doktor. | I am a doctor. |
| Yu es rafiki mi. | You are my friend. |
| Sol es anak. | Sol is a child. |
| Kita es familia. | We are a family. |
| Nama mi es Luma. | My name is Luma. |

## Before anything else: nothing

You have been doing this since Lesson 3. Nothing changes.

| | |
|---|---|
| Ruma kabir. | The house is big. |
| Ca garam. | The tea is hot. |
| Mi sini. | I am here. |
| Familia mi situ. | My family is there. |

## Why the difference

Put two nouns together and Amadunia already reads them: the second one owns the first.

| | |
|---|---|
| doktor rafiki mi | my friend's doctor |
| Rafiki mi **es** doktor. | My friend is a doctor. |

Without *es* you have said whose doctor. With *es* you have said what your friend is. An adjective cannot own anything, so it never needs the help.

## Was, will be

*es* is a verb. Put the tense word in front of it:

| | |
|---|---|
| Mi suda es anak. | I was a child. |
| Yu saufa es doktor. | You will be a doctor. |

Adjectives and place words take the tense word themselves — no *es*:

| | |
|---|---|
| Ruma suda kabir. | The house was big. |
| Ca saufa garam. | The tea will be hot. |
| Ta-ta suda sini. | They were here. |

## Asking

| | |
|---|---|
| Nama yu es ke? | What is your name? |
| Yu es kim? | Who are you? |
| Ta es doktor? | Is she a doctor? |
| Ruma kabir? | Is the house big? |

## A conversation

> — Salam! Nama yu es ke?
> — Nama mi es Sol. Mi es doktor. Yu?
> — Mi es rafiki Luma. Luma sini?
> — No, Luma situ. Ta suda es anak keci — sasa ta es doktor!
> — Ta hao?
> — Ya, ta cok hao.

*Hello! What's your name? — My name is Sol. I'm a doctor. You? — I'm Luma's friend. Is Luma here? — No, Luma is there. She was a small child — now she's a doctor! — Is she good? — Yes, she's very good.*

## Practice

1. Mi es insan. — *I am a person.*
2. Ain ta kabir. — *Her eyes are big.*
3. Yu-yu es familia? — *Are you a family?*
4. Ta suda es rafiki mi. — *He was my friend.*
5. Hotel es ruma? — *Is a hotel a house?*
6. Din saufa garam. — *The day will be hot.*

## What you can already say

Eleven lessons, 89 roots. You can say what anything is, was or will be, and what it is like — and you have learned exactly one word to do it.
